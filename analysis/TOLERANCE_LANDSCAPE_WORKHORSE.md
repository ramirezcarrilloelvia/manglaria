# Tolerance Landscapes ecological workhorse: implementation contract

## Purpose

Use observed estuarine time series to instantiate the formal objects in the static Tolerance Landscapes paper without introducing a dynamics on generator space.

Primary ecological candidate: Elkhorn Slough NERR, South Marsh, with Azevedo Pond, North Marsh and Vierra Mouth as within-estuary contrasts.

## Core empirical map

For each observational window `w`:

1. state: `X_t = dissolved oxygen`;
2. environmental condition: `Z_t = (water temperature, salinity, depth)`;
3. estimate `K_z^(w)(i,j) = P(X_{t+Delta}=j | X_t=i, Z_t=z)`;
4. compare `K^(w)` with a fixed reference generator using a perturbation-conditioned total-variation distance;
5. integrate each candidate generator with a fixed reference perturbation law `nu_ref` to obtain a marginal kernel;
6. evaluate one-step payoff, finite-horizon viable mass and conditional terminal-state diversity under a fixed initial-state distribution and oxygen-admissibility criterion.

Successive window estimates are **not** interpreted as a trajectory generated by a law on generator space. They are observational estimates placed in a common generator geometry.

## Primary outputs

- `conditioned_tv_to_reference`: response-architecture displacement;
- `marginal_tv_to_reference`: displacement after perturbation marginalization;
- `aliasing_excess = conditioned_tv - marginal_tv`: diagnostic of conditional architecture hidden by the baseline perturbation mixture;
- `expected_payoff_1step`;
- `viable_mass`;
- `terminal_entropy` and `effective_terminal_states`;
- raw-state summaries for direct comparison with the formal objects.

## Fixed-evaluation principle

Comparisons across windows hold fixed:

- bin definitions;
- the reference perturbation law `nu_ref`;
- initial-state distribution;
- viability threshold;
- horizon;
- distance definition.

This prevents a changing environmental mixture or changing state distribution from being mistaken for a change in response architecture.

## ManglarIA integration

The primary analysis should use observed data after QA/QC. Existing ManglarIA gap filling can be used in a separate sensitivity run, but imputed transitions should not enter the primary generator estimate without explicit labeling.

ManglarIA already provides:

- gap structure diagnostics;
- short-gap interpolation and bounded Random Forest gap filling;
- PSD / beta;
- Fisher information;
- functional connectivity and regime summaries.

## Alejandro Frank / MSI-TMSI bridge

Run A-Frank-Analysis on the same timestamped multivariate data and merge its sliding-window output by `window_center`.

Candidate external organization descriptors:

- leading eigenvalue;
- leading variance fraction;
- number of modes above Marchenko-Pastur;
- TMSI spectral exponent;
- leading temporal extent;
- lagged irreversibility `phi` and eigenvector tilt.

The main paper should initially carry only one external organization descriptor, preferably leading variance fraction or TMSI spectral exponent. The remainder belong in the supplement/reproducibility package.

## Main empirical falsification tests

1. **Same state, different architecture**: find windows with similar mean/current DO but large differences in conditioned generator displacement and/or viable mass.
2. **Same expected response, different viable futures**: identify windows with similar one-step payoff but different viable mass or future diversity.
3. **Perturbation aliasing**: identify generator pairs that are close after marginalization but separated conditionally on perturbation regime.
4. **Complexity non-redundancy**: test whether TL displacement is redundant with, or complementary to, the selected MSI/TMSI organization descriptor.
5. **External event alignment**: only after the above is defined, compare with independently recorded hypoxia, rain, bloom or weather events.

## Sensitivity analysis

At minimum vary:

- windows: 30, 60, 90 days;
- transition lag: 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 3 h;
- oxygen thresholds: 2, 3, 4, 5 mg/L;
- viability horizons: 6, 12, 24, 48 h;
- state and perturbation discretization;
- conditioned TV versus payoff-induced distance;
- observed-only versus gap-filled input.

No result should enter the paper solely because one specification looks visually compelling.
